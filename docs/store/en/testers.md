# Closed test: instructions for testers, English

What to send testers once they have opted in (the invite itself is in [`../../android-release.md`](../../android-release.md) §10). Paste each block into the Google Group's welcome message, a group post, or a chat. The Ukrainian version is [`uk/testers.md`](../uk/testers.md). Words in quotes are the app's own labels, so testers can find them on screen.

Never put a tester's name or email here: the repo is public. Log what they report, by label, in [`closed-test.md`](../closed-test.md).

## Testing instructions

```text
Thank you for testing Teach Clock! It's a game that teaches children aged 6–12 to read an analogue clock and set its hands.

1. Install the game from the Google Play link and keep it installed for 14 days. Google needs that for the game to go public. Updates come by themselves.
2. Open it, type a name and tap "Start".
3. In Settings pick a Game, a Mode and, where offered, the "Minute step" or "Hours". Then tap "Play".
4. Over the 14 days, try to play each of the four Games at least once:
   • Drag Hours: drag the numbers onto the empty face (try "Hours" 13–24 too).
   • Drag Minutes: place the minutes 5–60 around the face.
   • Drag Arrows: drag both hands to show the time asked for.
   • Set Time: read the clock and type the time.
5. Try both Modes: "Teach" (make a mistake on purpose and see how the game helps) and "Test" (no hints, results at the end).
6. If you can, also try: playing with a child; turning the phone sideways; the sun/moon button (light and dark theme); the УК/EN switch; airplane mode (the game should work fully offline); the phone's Back button in the middle of a Round.

No account, no ads, and nothing is collected: there is nothing to sign up for.
```

## What feedback do you want?

```text
Anything you notice helps, even a single sentence. Most useful:

• Did a child understand what to do without help? Where did they get stuck or bored?
• Anything that looked broken: text cut off, a button off the screen, a number that wouldn't drop where it should, a wrong answer counted as right (or the other way round).
• Did it ever close by itself, freeze or show a blank screen?
• Is anything confusing in the text, in either language?
• Your phone model and Android version (Settings › About phone), so I can reproduce problems.
• A screenshot if something looks wrong.

Send it by replying to this message, or with "Contact" at the bottom of the game's first screen.
```

## Known issues

```text
Already known, no need to report these (fixes are coming):

• On small phones the "Start" button on the first screen sits below the edge: scroll down to reach it.
• On a phone held sideways, the number pad in Set Time is partly below the edge: scroll to reach it.
• On phones, the confetti on the results screen can briefly cover the "Well done" heading.
• On older phones (Android 7–9) the game needs an up-to-date "Android System WebView". If the game looks broken, update it in Google Play.
```
